AI Hologram Purple — animated point-cloud adaptation

Source geometry: "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" by Lee Perry-Smith.
Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith
Based on work at www.triplegangers.com.

Changes: removed all surface textures; sampled rows of square light cells;
applied violet light, depth occlusion, cubic fractures and floating fragments.
Added digital eyes with gaze and blinking. Rigged head and neck independently
of the shoulders, with eased attention shifts and holds in a seamless 16-second loop.
Rendered with scripts/render-hologram.py.
